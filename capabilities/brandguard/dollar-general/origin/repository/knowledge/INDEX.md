# BRG08 knowledge index

This index records the current organization of the Dollar General BrandGuard
knowledge area. It is a repository map, not evidence that the content is
current, production-approved, or ready for publication without source review.

## Revision policy

- Each topic is stored as a DOCX/PDF pair.
- `v01d` is the provisional working revision for the topics listed below.
- `v01a` is retained historical/source material. It must not be deleted or
  replaced solely because a newer-looking revision exists.
- Before compiling an upload pack, recheck time-sensitive claims against the
  cited public sources and record the selected revision.
- A future canonical-revision decision should replace the word “provisional”
  here and in the repository guidance.

## File naming

The existing `BRG08-KNOW##-Topic-v##x` names are stable content identifiers.
They use title-style topic words for readability and are intentionally left
unchanged. Renaming them may affect upload manifests, external links, or
cross-surface references and requires a separate migration decision.
