# AskJamie-GPT-AJ04

**GPT-AJ04: AskJamie™ — OKHP³ BrandGuard™**

A comprehensive guardrail system for maintaining brand integrity, ethical standards, and consistent identity across all AskJamie interactions.

## Overview

GPT-AJ04 (BrandGuard™) implements three core guardrail components that work together to ensure AskJamie maintains appropriate tone, ethical guidelines, brand identity, and proper message framing:

### 🎯 **Tone Keeper**
Maintains AskJamie's distinctive voice and communication style across all interactions. Ensures responses are:
- Conversational yet professional
- Empathetic and supportive
- Consistent with brand personality
- Appropriately adapted to context

### ⚖️ **Ethics Sentinel**
Safeguards ethical boundaries and ensures responsible AI behavior. Monitors and prevents:
- Potentially harmful or misleading information
- Privacy violations
- Bias and discrimination
- Content that conflicts with ethical guidelines

### 🧭 **Framing Navigator**
Guides how topics and information are presented to align with brand values and messaging strategy. Ensures:
- Brand identity consistency
- Message alignment with organizational values
- Appropriate context and perspective
- Strategic communication framing

# AskJamie™ — GPT‑AJ04: OKHP³ BrandGuard™

**Guardrails for tone, ethics, and identity — at GPT speed.**

This repository is the canonical storage space for **GPT‑AJ04 (BrandGuard)**: AskJamie’s dedicated lens that reviews **language, style, and moral framing** before anything leaves your drafts.

---

## What BrandGuard includes
BrandGuard is a suite of lenses inside a single GPT:

- **Tone Keeper** — Checks cadence, vocabulary, and energy; helps prevent drift into “generic AI voice,” and suggests on‑brand rewrites.
- **Ethics Sentinel** — Flags potentially harmful, biased, or exclusionary language and proposes inclusive alternatives.
- **Framing Navigator** — Clarifies intent vs. stakeholder impact; surfaces hidden trade‑offs and offers clearer, more honest framing.

---

## What BrandGuard does with your prompts
Use GPT‑AJ04 as the calm reviewer between your people, your GPTs, and your channels:

- **Before you write:** Co‑design brand‑safe prompt patterns, content frames, disclaimers, and do/don’t rules.
- **While you write:** Review drafts, transcripts, or flows for tone drift, over‑promising, and ethics risks; get safer alternatives.
- **Before you ship:** Run a “BrandGuard pass” on campaigns, sales sequences, onboarding journeys, and GPT instructions.
- **After launch:** Periodically audit snippets, macros, chat flows, and governance docs so your GPT footprint keeps sounding like you.
