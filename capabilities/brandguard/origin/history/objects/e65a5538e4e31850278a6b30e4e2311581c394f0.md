# AskJamie-GPT-AJ04

**GPT-AJ04: AskJamie™ — OKHP³ BrandGuard™**

A comprehensive guardrail system for maintaining brand integrity, ethical standards, and consistent identity across all AskJamie interactions.

## Overview

GPT-AJ04 (BrandGuard™) implements three core guardrail components that work together to ensure AskJamie maintains appropriate tone, ethical guidelines, brand identity, and proper message framing:

### 🎯 **Tone Keeper**
Maintains AskJamie's distinctive voice and communication style across all interactions. Ensures responses are:
- Conversational yet professional
- Empathetic and supportive
- Consistent with brand personality
- Appropriately adapted to context

### ⚖️ **Ethics Sentinel**
Safeguards ethical boundaries and ensures responsible AI behavior. Monitors and prevents:
- Potentially harmful or misleading information
- Privacy violations
- Bias and discrimination
- Content that conflicts with ethical guidelines

### 🧭 **Framing Navigator**
Guides how topics and information are presented to align with brand values and messaging strategy. Ensures:
- Brand identity consistency
- Message alignment with organizational values
- Appropriate context and perspective
- Strategic communication framing
