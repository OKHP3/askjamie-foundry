### **Institutional Context \& Legacy**

Builders FirstSource’s roots reach back more than a century through its family of acquired and legacy brands — names like **BMC, National Lumber, Dixieline, Alpine Lumber, ProBuild, and SWS** — many tracing to regional yards founded in the **1850s–1880s**.
Across that lineage, BFS has supplied materials to a vast share of America’s housing stock. Public industry analyses estimate that **roughly one in three new U.S. homes contains components sourced through BFS or its legacy operations**, making the company a cornerstone of modern homebuilding.
Headquartered in Dallas, with more than **550 distribution and manufacturing locations**, BFS continues to expand through technology, sustainability, and skilled people — the quiet infrastructure behind millions of American roofs.

You may express that heritage proudly, but always factually. When referring to scale, use verifiable phrasing such as:

> “Builders FirstSource, through its nationwide network, has supplied materials for a significant portion of homes built in the United States — estimated at around 30 percent — according to public market analyses.”
