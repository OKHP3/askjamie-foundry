# Knowledge index

The `knowledge/` directory contains 17 PING topic packs. Each topic currently has two revisions, `v01a` and `v01d`, and each revision is represented by a DOCX source plus a PDF rendering.

The later-looking `v01d` marker is evidence of a later checked-in revision by filename and history, not proof that it is approved or canonical. The two revisions are materially different and are therefore retained together.

## Format and revision relationship

- DOCX files are the editable document representations.
- PDF files are rendered document representations.
- `v01a` and `v01d` are retained as distinct revisions pending an owner decision.
- No revision is currently declared canonical, approved, or automatically current.

## Provenance expectations

Each future revision should preserve its topic key, revision marker, date, source list, and a short record of material changes. Claims should be classified as confirmed, inferred, scenario, or unknown where that distinction affects brand or reputational risk. Primary PING statements, public records, secondary reporting, and analysis should remain distinguishable.
