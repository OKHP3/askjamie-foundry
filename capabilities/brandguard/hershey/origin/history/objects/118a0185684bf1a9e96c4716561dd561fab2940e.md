## Understanding

### Purpose

**Confirmed:** The repository supports an OpenAI/ChatGPT Custom GPT intended to monitor and explain Hershey brand compliance. It stores brand knowledge and the planned prompt, retrieval, evaluation, operations, build, and release surfaces.

**Inferred:** The main value is a reusable, evidence-backed BrandGuard workflow that can identify brand-related issues, explain them, and support safer brand decisions. The actual monitor behavior is not implemented in this repository yet.

### Mission and vision

- **Repository mission, inferred:** Maintain public-source Hershey knowledge and the surrounding AI assets needed to evaluate and operate a BrandGuard sentinel.
- **Longer-term vision, inferred:** A dependable Custom GPT that can answer Hershey brand questions, identify product and messaging risks, and communicate findings with citations and appropriate uncertainty.
- **Important boundary:** The knowledge files describe Hershey's public mission, values, products, and risks. They are not official internal Hershey brand standards, and this repository is not affiliated with or endorsed by Hershey.

### Users and workflows

- **Confirmed:** The assets are organized for a Custom GPT and retrieval-augmented knowledge use.
- **Inferred users:** Brand reviewers, content or marketing teams, product and retail support users, and maintainers of the AskJamie / BrandGuard suite.
- **Expected workflow, not yet verified end to end:** curate public sources, maintain knowledge packs, add system prompts and retrieval corpus assets, define evaluations, then prepare a release for the Custom GPT.

## Safe change conventions

- Work from the repository root and preserve unrelated user changes.
- Keep `AGENTS.md` as the canonical agent guide. `CLAUDE.md` currently points to it and should remain short unless Claude-specific rules are genuinely needed.
- Treat `knowledge/` as public-source reference material. Preserve citations, source limitations, non-affiliation notices, and uncertainty statements.
- Treat `v01a` and `v01d` as distinct versioned artifacts. Do not delete, merge, or silently replace one with the other.
- Do not present public-source summaries as official Hershey policy or as a substitute for current official guidance.
- Do not add secrets, private Notion data, credentials, or unverified production claims.
- Keep prompts, RAG sources, evaluations, compiled resources, and release artifacts in their designated directories rather than mixing them into `knowledge/`.
- When adding executable tooling, add its dependencies, entry point, validation command, and runtime assumptions to this guide.
- `.github/dependabot.yml` and `.github/workflows/technology-freshness.yml` maintain future action/package references and flag Nix stable-channel drift. The freshness workflow uses only the GitHub-hosted runner, `curl`, `sed`, `grep`, `sort`, and GitHub CLI; it does not add a BRG06 application runtime.
