# Knowledge forge workflow

Status: reviewed method distilled from S01-S17, S18, S20, S23, S26, and the recovered BrandGuard prompt library plan. This is an original portable workflow, not a claim that its stages ran successfully.

## Identify before researching

Record the intended brand, legal name, operating name, domain, jurisdiction, and available authoritative identifiers. Preserve the exact legal spelling alongside a normalized matching form. A search match or similar name is a candidate, not acceptance evidence.

Build a candidate list for parent, subsidiary, division, historical entity, and similarly named companies. Use independent authoritative evidence, including a primary source appropriate to the field, before accepting identity. Record why candidates were accepted or rejected. Domain certificates and directory listings alone do not establish legal identity. Do not require unavailable identifiers or invent values for a private company.

Separate operational founding dates from incorporation dates. Related website links do not by themselves establish common legal ownership. Missing values remain null with an explicit reason. Changed identifiers trigger review of dependent topics.

## Source and draft in bounded stages

The recovered historical library described five stages: schema/front matter, data harvest, composition, validation/CanonSeal, and export. Retain those useful boundaries while treating historical word counts, page targets, browsing modes, and Canvas requirements as design suggestions.

1. **Scope:** choose one topic, entity, time window, user jobs, and exclusions. Read its existing pair and source register before drafting.
2. **Evidence:** collect public sources with title, URL, source type, retrieval date, publication/event date, supported claim, and limitations. Prefer the appropriate primary evidence; preserve useful secondary context with attribution.
3. **Composition:** organize facts by the chosen topic. Keep historical knowledge, analysis, proposals, and unknowns distinguishable. Use short sections and crosslinks rather than repeating the whole company profile in every file.
4. **Review:** check entity alignment, dates, citations, unsupported numbers, contradictions, sensitive material, and practical usefulness. A review summary should list supported conclusions, assumptions, gaps, and follow-up work. The historical name CanonSeal is not independent certification.
5. **Export:** generate paired formats only after substantive approval, retain metadata and disclosures, inspect rendering and extraction, hash the results, and record the exact reviewed version.

## Topic contract

Each new topic should include a stable topic key, title, version, entity reference, status, creation/update dates, author, unofficial-resource disclosure, sensitivity, and retrieval guidance. The body needs purpose, relevant content, user questions, crosslinks, sources, limitations, and change notes. All time-sensitive conclusions need an as-of date.

The historical library covered 16 topics. KNOW17 adds a deliberately restricted reputation module. Use the observed 17-topic index for this repository; do not silently renumber files to match older plans.

## Expansion prompt

Use this original, bounded replacement for the repeated Phase 2 requests:

> Review the supplied BRG12 topic and its sources. Confirm the entity and intended time window. Inventory distinct reusable value before writing. Identify unsupported or stale claims and separate them from documented facts. Expand only where additional primary evidence, explanation, comparison, or a practical example adds value. Preserve citations and unresolved conflicts. Label scenarios and SCAMPER ideas as proposals. Return a revised draft, claim/source table, changes from the prior version, missing assets, and precise validation steps. Do not claim current verification, full preservation, file creation, or publication unless supported by actual receipts.

Historical Phase 2 prompts requested 20,000-25,000 words, broad causal analysis, 30-50 questions, scenario branches, SCAMPER, and repeated company confirmation. User replies consistently asked to proceed with deeper breadth and nuance. The two expansion rounds are separate historical work. Their 33 truncated assistant items are not assumed duplicated by the original corpus. No word count is a quality gate for this revised workflow.

## Decision methods and safeguards

- **Options and criteria:** show alternatives, benefits, costs, evidence, and the reason for a recommendation. These are visible decision aids.
- **SCAMPER:** use Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to another use, Eliminate, and Reverse to generate labeled ideas. Ideas are not company plans.
- **Equilibrium review:** establish facts and constraints, test a meaningful contrary case, then reconcile against evidence. Record who or what actually performed the checks. Do not claim independent review from role labels alone.
- **Sensitive material:** distinguish every issue's procedural status, affected entity, dates, remediation, source strength, and missing evidence. Explain relevance without amplification. Uncalibrated severity or recurrence scores are historical heuristics, not measured probabilities.

## Completion evidence

A draft is ready for review when sources are traceable, conflicts remain visible, the original pair is preserved, rich elements have a destination or missing-asset entry, and another maintainer can resume without the source chat. Publication, runtime adoption, legal clearance, and owner acceptance remain separate decisions.
