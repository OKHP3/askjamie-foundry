# Knowledge corpus review and retrieval guide

Reviewed 2026-10-16. This is a historical corpus inventory and use guide, not current company research. All 17 source DOCX/PDF pairs remain unchanged. Both formats were parsed for text and metadata. Selected content and source lists were inspected; no full factual revalidation or visual-layout QA was performed.

Each source reports version 1.0, canonical status, and creation/update dates of 2026-01-02. Canonical is the source's internal designation. It does not establish current accuracy. The source files preserve public-source citations and an unofficial educational disclosure. New Markdown companions retain their content for portable reading and search, with explicit historical warnings. Tables and paragraph order are transcribed; visual fidelity and footnote layout are not claimed.

## Conflicts and freshness work

- **Ownership:** KNOW01 describes family ownership; KNOW05 describes sole ownership and CEO status. Do not silently reconcile different dates, terms, or scopes.
- **Founding and incorporation:** source drafts describe a 1992 operating origin and a separate 1995 legal creation date. Corpus founding-location wording differs between Austin, Minnesota and Wisconsin. Keep event types and geographic claims distinct until reconciled.
- **Facility scale and mission:** square-footage and mission phrasing vary. Use dated authoritative evidence before quoting a single canonical value.
- **Products and performance:** model names, release years, dimensions, speed ratings, compatibility, and current/discontinued status require verification. An older catalog or nominal rating is not current availability or an individual performance guarantee.
- **Corporate relationships:** distinguish a brand, division, subsidiary, acquisition, and commercial partnership. Related links alone do not prove a legal relationship.
- **Ownership/financial estimates:** never convert commercial-directory estimates or assistant inferences into audited financial facts. Missing stock or ownership data remains unknown unless verified.
- **ESG and loyalty:** source proposals and industry analogies do not establish a company program. Verify a named initiative or benefit before presenting it as available.
- **Future outlook:** separate documented plans from scenarios and SCAMPER proposals. Relative dates and rolling five-year windows refer to the source's period.
- **Source citations:** the original pairs contain source references and URLs. Chat research responses include unresolved citation markers. Preserve those markers privately as evidence; do not invent their destinations.

## Restricted use of KNOW17

Use only for explicit questions about controversies, litigation, recalls, safety, ethics, or negative press. Do not surface it for unrelated product or marketing questions. Preserve distinctions among allegations, reports, investigations, settlements, adjudications, remediation, and ongoing status. Verify current procedural status and affected entity before a current answer. The source's severity and recurrence heuristics are not calibrated forecasts.

## What the companions establish

The companion checker proves that Markdown matches the deterministic DOCX transcription under its documented formatting rules. It does not prove DOCX/PDF equivalence, citation validity, current facts, correct model behavior, or completeness of the Project's later expansions.

The two Phase 2 rounds in each KNOW thread remain separate recovery work. Thirty-three assistant message items were truncated by the supported reader. Existing January pairs and these text companions do not replace those unassessed bodies. Original thread attachments require identity checks before any equivalence claim.
