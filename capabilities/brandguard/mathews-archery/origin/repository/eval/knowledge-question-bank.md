# Knowledge question bank

Status: original question design distilled from S01's newly recovered rendered FAQ text on 2026-10-16. Questions are retrieval and evaluation needs, not verified answers. No model runs performed.

The later FAQ responses add practical comparisons, maintenance questions, warranty and compatibility questions, buying criteria, and a second broader company FAQ. Reuse their question patterns without promoting unsupported dates, prices, performance, or program claims. A prompt containing a technology name does not establish that the technology exists.

| Intent | Reusable question | Evidence requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Identify | When did the operating business begin, and how does that differ from legal incorporation? | Distinct dated events and entity records |
| Explain | What problem did a documented technology address? | Official description plus clearly attributed limitations |
| Compare | What changed between two exact bow models and years? | Like-for-like model specifications and source dates |
| Compare | Which differences matter for this user's hunting or target use? | User constraints, documented features, and fitting limitations |
| Compare | How were noise or vibration differences measured? | Method, conditions, units, and evidence quality |
| Compare | What can a nominal speed rating tell us, and what can it not tell us? | Rating conditions and limits of individualized inference |
| Clarify | How do axle length, brace height, mass, draw length, draw weight, and let-off differ? | Correct definitions and model-specific verified values |
| Clarify | What distinguishes a cam platform from a module or accessory? | Manufacturer terminology and model/year context |
| Compatibility | Does this exact accessory fit this bow model and year? | Explicit compatibility evidence; no inference from brand name alone |
| Compatibility | How can a left-handed or shorter-draw archer check available configurations? | Current official catalog or authorized dealer confirmation |
| Compatibility | How do youth products and related brands differ from the main model range? | Verified product and legal/brand relationships |
| Maintenance | What should an owner ask a qualified technician before changing modules or draw weight? | Correct manual and qualified service route |
| Maintenance | Which adjustments require a press or professional service on this exact model? | Model-specific official instructions and safety boundaries |
| Maintenance | What routine inspection guidance is documented? | Current manual; no hazardous modification guidance |
| Safety | How should a user route a concern about limbs, strings, or a suspected recall? | Current official safety notice and affected-product identification |
| Warranty | What warranty terms apply to this model, purchase date, and owner? | Current official terms and support confirmation |
| Warranty | How do competitor warranties differ without assuming equal coverage? | Dated complete policies and exclusions |
| Purchase | What should a buyer ask about total accessory and service costs? | Current quotes where available; historical prices labeled |
| Purchase | Is there documented evidence of a loyalty or referral program? | Named official program or explicit unknown status |
| Support | Where can users find an authorized retailer or official support? | Verified current route and no implied endorsement of third-party listings |
| Community | Which conservation or community activities are documented for a stated period? | Dated first-party evidence and correct partner attribution |
| Scope | Does a source call an internal review label independent certification? | Explain method versus independent evidence |
| Outlook | Is this future statement a documented plan, an analysis, or an idea? | Evidence tier and date; proposals kept separate |
| Governance | Do two topic files disagree on the same fact? | Source reconciliation rather than majority vote |

The rendered source includes questionable or insufficiently supported technology terminology, current-price examples, equipment-based claims about target panic, and a final claim of verified up-to-date knowledge. These require review. They are not accepted facts or medical, legal, financial, or safety guidance in this bank.
