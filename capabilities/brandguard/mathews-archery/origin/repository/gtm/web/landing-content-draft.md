## What it is

An unofficial educational reference project from OverKill Hill P³, exploring how public-source AI can explain Mathews Archery with care, clarity, and traceable evidence.

The aim is practical: help an archer understand a term, compare documented features, prepare dealer questions, and find the appropriate official source.

## Why it exists

A familiar name can become an inaccurate answer when sources are mixed, product years disappear, or confident language outruns evidence. BrandGuard studies how to reduce those errors through a clear entity record, dated knowledge, useful explanations, and explicit limits.

This repository preserves the research and design lineage. Portable instructions and future agent capabilities require review and testing before adoption.

## Try these questions

- What should I compare when choosing between two model years?
- Explain this technology in plain language and show the dated source.
- Is this source describing the intended company or a similarly named business?
- What do I need to ask an authorized dealer about fit and service?

For useful comparisons, bring the exact models and years, intended use, experience, and constraints. Share only the information needed for the question.

## Principles

**Brand integrity:** keep names, facts, and sources aligned.

**Customer trust:** expose uncertainty and avoid promises the evidence cannot support.

**Safety and care:** route real-world fitting, repair, warranty, and purchase decisions to official channels and qualified people.
