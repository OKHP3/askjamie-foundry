### 1. Introduction & Brand Context

Mathews Archery is a U.S.‑based manufacturer of high‑performance compound bows and related accessories. Founded in 1992 by engineer and bow designer Matt McPherson, the company introduced the Solocam single‑cam system, which simplified timing and reduced recoil while delivering high energy. McPherson started Mathews in Austin, Minnesota, but within a few years the growing business relocated to Sparta, Wisconsin to access more space, a skilled workforce and supportive community[1]. The company’s commitment to research, innovation and precision manufacturing made it one of the largest‑grossing bow manufacturers in North America, expanding from just two employees to over 400 and producing more than a million bows[2]. Mathews designs and builds every bow in‑house: engineers design risers and cams, raw billets are machined, dipped and finished, strings and cables are assembled, and final bows are built and tested in two facilities in Sparta[3].

- Team Mathews Contingency Program – a structured loyalty program paying monetary rewards to shooters who place at sanctioned events with Mathews equipment. The 2025 program offers over US$1.5 million in payouts and lists qualification rules such as using Mathews bows and Bridge‑Lock stabilizers, registering before events and attending award ceremonies[5]. While not a consumer rewards program in the traditional sense, it fosters loyalty among competitive shooters.

- Customer service & warranty – Mathews offers a limited lifetime warranty on risers, limbs and cams for the original owner when purchased from an authorised dealer. Strings and cables carry a one‑year warranty.

Mathews does not operate a points‑based loyalty program for recreational customers; instead it invests in high‑touch service through its dealer network and supports brand ambassadors and professional teams.
