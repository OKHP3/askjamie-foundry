### 1. Introduction & Brand Context

Mathews Archery is a privately owned American bow manufacturer based in Sparta, Wisconsin. The company was founded in 1992 by inventor Matt McPherson, who introduced the single‑cam “SoloCam” compound bow that simplified synchronization and delivered a smoother draw[^16]. Remaining privately held has allowed McPherson to make quick decisions and pursue long‑term innovations without the pressure of quarterly earnings[^3]. Today Mathews designs and manufactures high‑performance compound bows and accessories for bowhunters and competitive target archers worldwide.

Alongside product innovation, Mathews promotes a culture built on “Innovation, Integrity and Impact.” Company materials explain that these values guide product design and philanthropic efforts; Mathews helped develop the Genesis bow for the National Archery in the Schools Program (NASP), enabling millions of students to try archery, and supports conservation projects and humanitarian causes such as digging wells and producing the Light of the World film[^19]. In recent years Mathews and its media partners have highlighted community initiatives: during a 2024 event in Atlanta known as The Venison Project, volunteers harvested deer and donated more than 700 pounds of processed venison to local shelters and food banks[^18].

Despite largely positive press, Mathews is one of several bow manufacturers named in a 2025 class‑action lawsuit alleging that manufacturers, distributors and major retailers conspired to fix prices through enforcement of Minimum Advertised Price (MAP) policies. Law firm coverage of the suit notes that the Archery Trade Association is accused of organizing MAP enforcement and that the plaintiffs allege the policies artificially inflated prices for consumers[^22][^23]. Archery media highlight that MAP policies can protect independent pro‑shops and consumers when enforced unilaterally, but the legal challenge has nonetheless become part of the brand’s recent public narrative.

### 5. Sustainability & ESG Commitments

Mathews does not publish a formal sustainability report, but several initiatives demonstrate the company’s social and ethical commitments:

- Innovation, Integrity & Impact values: Company materials emphasize that Mathews strives to create innovative products, operate with integrity and positively impact the world. The philanthropic ethos stems from founder Matt McPherson’s Christian faith and personal mission[^19].

- Youth outreach & education: Mathews worked with other stakeholders to design the Genesis bow for NASP, enabling millions of students to draw a bow regardless of strength or size[^19]. The company supports archery clubs and provides equipment for school programs.

- Community giving & conservation: Mathews and partners like Seek One host events such as The Venison Project, where volunteers harvest deer and donate processed meat to homeless shelters; the 2024 event yielded over 700 pounds of venison for local food banks[^18]. Mathews and the McPherson family have funded missionary work, drilled wells and supported international ministries, and they sponsor the Light of the World film[^19].

- American manufacturing: Media reports emphasise that Mathews bows are designed and built in the U.S. USA Archery’s partnership renewal highlighted Mathews’ commitment to building bows in America and supporting the U.S. archery community[^17]. This domestic manufacturing reduces shipping distances compared with overseas production and contributes to local employment.

Because Mathews is privately held, there is limited public information on its environmental footprint, carbon emissions or supply‑chain transparency. No comprehensive environmental targets or ESG metrics were found in public sources during this research. Future updates may be needed if the company releases sustainability reports.
