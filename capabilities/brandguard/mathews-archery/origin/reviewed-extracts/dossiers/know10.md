### 1. Introduction & Brand Context

Mathews Archery (sometimes shortened to Mathews Inc.) is an American bow manufacturer headquartered in Sparta, Wisconsin. Founded in 1992 by entrepreneur and engineer Matt McPherson, the company pioneered the SoloCam single‑cam compound bow. The brand’s stated aim is to “elevate the archery experience”[1], and its portfolio has grown from one handmade prototype to a wide range of hunting and target bows, accessories, and youth programs. Mathews positions itself as an innovation‑driven, family‑owned company that manufactures every bow domestically and maintains direct relationships with independent pro shops and dealers[2]. This customer‑first ethos is reinforced by core values of Innovation, Integrity, and Impact, which founder McPherson credits to his Christian faith and commitment to giving back[3].

#### Market positioning & customer loyalty

The compound‑bow market is price‑intensive - flagship bows often exceed US$1,300 - and dominated by niche brands. Mathews maintains a premium positioning but fosters strong loyalty through:

- Team Mathews ambassadors. The company sponsors top professional archers such as Levi Morgan and Kyle Douglas. At the 2025 ASA Classic, Team Mathews won five pro classes and four Shooter‑of‑the‑Year titles[8], reinforcing the brand’s competitive reputation. Influencers share tips and hunts, creating a community around the brand[1].

- Brand callout and culture. Outdoor Life notes that Mathews’ tagline “Elevating the archery experience” (and earlier slogan “Catch us if you can”) fosters a team mentality among owners; customers are willing to pay more for accessories bearing the Mathews logo[1]. This sense of belonging enhances loyalty and reduces price sensitivity.

- Integrated service and warranty. By controlling production and requiring purchases through certified dealers, Mathews ensures that customers receive personalized setup, service and warranty support[2][15]. The limited lifetime warranty encourages long‑term relationships with dealers and keeps used equipment within authorized channels.

#### Loyalty programs and community engagement

Mathews does not operate a points‑based consumer loyalty program; instead, its loyalty strategy revolves around community‑building, contingency incentives, and pro‑staff ambassadors:

- Contingency programs. To incentivize competitive shooters, Mathews offers a contingency program with monetary payouts for podium finishes at ASA, IBO and NFAA events; the 2025 program totals over US$1.5 million[11]. Participants must wear Mathews apparel at awards and meet registration requirements. There is also a Bridge‑Lock Stabilizer contingency for shooters using Mathews’ integrated stabilizer system[11]. These programs reward loyalty and encourage archers to compete with Mathews equipment.

- Youth engagement and charitable initiatives. Mathews invests in youth archery through the NASP raffle program[9] and philanthropic donations to breast‑cancer research[13]. By tying charitable giving to product purchases and raffles, Mathews fosters goodwill and long‑term brand affinity among families and communities.
