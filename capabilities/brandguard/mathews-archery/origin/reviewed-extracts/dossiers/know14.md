### 1. Introduction & Brand Context

Mathews Archery is an independently owned bow manufacturer headquartered in Sparta, Wisconsin. The firm was founded in 1992 by Matt McPherson, an inventor who started building compound bows at age 13 and later patented the “InnerCam” system before launching Mathews Archery[1]. The company’s ethos is built on three core values  -  innovation, integrity and impact  -  which McPherson and his team stress as non‑negotiable guiding principles[2]. McPherson and his early employees turned a two‑person operation that sold 175 bows in its first year into a business employing over 400 people, producing hundreds of bows daily and holding more than 200 patented or patent‑pending innovations[3]. Mathews is widely regarded as the largest‑grossing bow manufacturer, and it is known for game‑changing technologies such as single‑cam Solocam® designs, parallel limb configurations and the Genesis bow used in the National Archery in the Schools Program (NASP).

### 7. Industry Trends / Partnerships / Collaborations

#### Industry Participation & Trends

- Rebound from COVID‑19 – The Archery Trade Association’s summit notes that bowhunter education program participation dropped from over 160,000 students in 2019 to 121,295 in 2020 due to COVID‑19, but rebounded to 178,208 students in 2022[58]. The number of bowhunter education classes offered dipped from 544 in 2019 to 262 in 2020, rising again to 298 by 2022[59]. Similarly, NASP participation returned to pre‑pandemic levels, reaching 8,449 schools in 2022[60]. These data points suggest resilience and pent‑up demand that will benefit manufacturers like Mathews.

- Youth Participation & NASP – The U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service’s 2025 addendum on target shooting notes that archery participation grew from 7 million in 2000 to over 19 million by 2025[61]. Participation is highest among youth ages 6–15, a trend attributed to NASP’s reach of 23.5 million students across 9,500 schools[62]. Such growth in youth engagement provides a pipeline of future customers for premium equipment.
