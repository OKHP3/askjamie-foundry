### 1. Introduction & Brand Context

Mathews Archery is a privately held archery‑equipment manufacturer known for innovating high‑performance compound bows. Founder Matt McPherson designed his first stick bow at age ten and a compound bow at thirteen and later invented Solocam technology[1]. He started McPherson Archery in 1985 and launched Mathews Archery in 1992, believing that owning the company outright allowed him to make decisions quickly and push new technologies[2]. The company is headquartered in Sparta, Wisconsin and has grown from two employees in 1992 to hundreds of employees, becoming one of Sparta’s largest employers[3][4]. Mathews is a family of bowhunters, inventors and storytellers who design and manufacture every part of their bows in‑house[5], emphasizing quality, innovation and customer service.

Mathews’ mission is built on three core values - Innovation, Integrity and Impact - and the company stresses continuous improvement, simplified yet advanced engineering and giving back[6]. The brand’s early marketing slogan “speed without the kick” captured its focus on producing smooth‑shooting bows with minimal vibration[7]. The company’s Genesis bow, introduced over 20 years ago, became the official bow of the National Archery in the Schools Program (NASP) and helped bring archery to millions of students[8]. Today Mathews sells a portfolio of hunting and target bows, manufactures accessories and operates the value‑oriented Mission Archery division for affordable bows[9]. Mathews also partners with conservation organizations and invests in community impact projects such as the Salvation Poem project and clean‑water initiatives[10].

### 5. Sustainability & ESG Commitments

Mathews does not publish comprehensive ESG reports; however, the company promotes stewardship, community impact and charitable initiatives. Its core values include Integrity and Impact, and the brand supports both conservation and humanitarian projects.

- Conservation & NASP – Mathews partnered with the Kentucky Department of Fish & Wildlife in 2002 to create what became the National Archery in the Schools Program (NASP). The program now involves 1.3 million students in over 9,500 schools, teaching archery skills and encouraging outdoor participation[14]. ### 6. Consumer Experience & Loyalty Systems

Mathews emphasizes customer service, working through authorized dealers who provide personalized setup, tuning and repair. The company offers a limited lifetime warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship for the original registered owner. Owners must register their bow within three months of purchase; warranty claims must be processed through an authorized Mathews retailer[22]. The warranty does not cover normal wear, misuse or dry fires, and retailers may charge service fees related to warranty work[22].

Mathews maintains strong brand loyalty through community engagement (NASP, humanitarian projects), premium product quality and a network of dealers offering service and guidance. The introduction of digital tools like Shot Sense strengthens the connection with users by providing analytics and social features[20].
