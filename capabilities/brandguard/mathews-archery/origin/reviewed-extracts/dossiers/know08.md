### 1. Introduction & Brand Context

Mathews Archery is a privately held U.S. manufacturer of premium compound bows and archery accessories. The company was founded in 1992 by bow designer Matt McPherson in Sparta, Wisconsin, and quickly built its reputation on Solocam technology - single‑cam systems that simplified tuning and improved shooting feel. McPherson built his first compound bow by age 13 and later invented Solocam technology; he established Mathews to pursue “the ultimate archery experience” and describes the brand as a family of bowhunters, inventors and storytellers[1]. Today Mathews designs and builds both hunting and target bows in the United States and distributes them through independent retailers around the world[2]. The company’s mission remains focused on engineering innovations that give bowhunters and competitive archers a performance edge while supporting conservation and archery participation.

Mathews is widely regarded as one of the largest manufacturers of advanced target and hunting bows[2]. Beyond product innovation, the company invests in community‑building programs. In 2001 it partnered with the Kentucky Departments of Fish & Wildlife Resources and Education to co‑create the National Archery in the Schools Program (NASP), which launched in 21 Kentucky schools in 2002 and has since grown nationally[3]. Mathews also maintains a division called Mission Archery (established in 2007) that offers more affordable bows built around core Mathews technologies[4]. These initiatives demonstrate the brand’s commitment to expanding the sport while preserving its hunting heritage.
