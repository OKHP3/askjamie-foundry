## D03. Keep scenarios and unknown evidence explicit

Record decision, hypothetical trigger, source-attributed premise, inputs with units, alternatives, benefits, costs, constraints, observable indicator, evidence needed, owner, and what would reject an option. Scenarios are not company plans or forecasts.

The historical channel tree has two branches:

| Branch | Potential benefits in the source | Costs and constraints | Example sub-options |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct-to-consumer emphasis | Margin, direct feedback, pricing agility | Platform and logistics investment, fulfillment complexity, dealer conflict | Premium versus affordable offering; limited run or selected-dealer mitigation |
| Dealer emphasis | Demonstration, fitting, service, existing relationships | Less direct margin, slower feedback, missed online opportunity | Specialist premium service versus a budget offer bundled with lessons |

Compare both against an observable demand signal, competing explanation, competitor action, distribution consequence, and feedback rule. Weak sell-through can challenge an option; a plausible narrative does not prove it.

The source's numerical stress inputs include a 10% selling-price decrease, a 5% accessory mix shift, a 5% annual participation decline, a 20% sales decline over five years, doubled exports, a hypothetical competitor taking 10% share, and a 10% sustainability premium. These are historical hypothetical inputs, not observed changes, forecasts, probabilities, or recommended planning assumptions. Record the baseline and units before any calculation. No calculation or business forecast has been run here.

Keep growth and plateau branches distinct. Growth options raise capacity, training, engineering, quality, and culture questions. Plateau options raise excess capacity, product focus, adjacent-market, trade-in, channel, and dealer-conflict questions. No branch proves management intent.

For private-company financial evidence, retain entity, metric, currency, period, provider, estimate method, and disclosure limits. Divergent third-party estimates do not bound the truth. An illustrative profit case is not company profit. Missing funding or debt news does not establish zero debt or financial health. Sale and IPO trade-offs remain scenarios unless actual evidence establishes a plan.

## D04. Separate ESG measurement from illustration and proposal

Use five evidence classes: attributed measured report, inference, generic benchmark, illustrative model, and proposed target. Record facility/product scope, metric, unit, period, baseline, comparison value, functional unit, system boundary, method, source, missing inputs, conflicts, owner, and acceptance test.

The source has an instructive metric conflict: a reclamation result is described first as residual oil and later as waste moisture, using the same 15% to less-than-1% figures. Keep both assertions with a conflict key. Verify the actual source's metric and denominator before interpreting a result. Fluid retained in scrap, scrap reject rate, throughput, electricity, and emissions are different measures.

A generic material footprint per kilogram is not a bow life-cycle footprint. A conceptual per-bow allocation is not a measured company LCA. Preserve geography, energy mix, carbon-storage treatment, end-of-life boundary, and estimate markers. Do not sum illustrative numbers and publish them as company performance.

The source proposes mutually inconsistent 2030 reductions and renewable/packaging targets. Keep them as alternatives with missing baseline, owner, feasibility, deadline, and measurement plan. A federation's target does not transfer through an alleged partnership. Conservation giving or a family-culture narrative does not establish environmental or workforce outcomes.

## D05. Type the relationship before describing it

| Relationship type | What to record separately |
|---|---|
| Product co-development or integration | Each party's contribution, product/version, interface, branding, compatibility scope |
| Licensing | IP owner, licensee, direction, subject, term, rights, unknown payment conditions |
| Dealer distribution/service | Manufacturer support and dealer services, program date, independence, eligibility |
| Media sponsorship or athlete relationship | Counterparty, contract/period evidence, supplied support, separate public roster |
| Open contingency program | Participant eligibility and program terms; not proof of a sponsorship contract |
| Acquisition, divestiture, internal brand creation | One event row per change, affected entity, control, effective dates, actual legal evidence |

The source's accessory collaboration examples do not prove ownership. An inward license and an outward license have different roles. Use of a bow at a tournament does not prove a current contract. A shared founder, factory, technology, or brand name does not establish a parent/subsidiary relationship. Forum rumors and competitor transactions do not establish a Mathews transaction.

Record subject entity, disambiguated counterparty, precise type, product/program/IP scope, contributions or rights, announcement date, effective start/end dates, source and date, historical/current/proposed status, separately evidenced ownership, conflicts, and next document needed. Preserve contradictory descriptions rather than choosing the most convenient label.

## D06. Reconcile dated claims without flattening them

Use one row per asserted event with a shared conflict-group key. Keep invention, patent, announcement, model year, first production use, and later adoption as different date types. Record the exact model/technology/entity, claimed significance, source attribution, event/publication/retrieval dates, uncertainties, conflicts, and required evidence.

The historical sources illustrate why this matters:

- A late-2019 launch for a 2020 model year can describe two compatible dates.
- Different Reezen, V3X, NO CAM, and CrossCentric assertions may reflect event-type differences, naming ambiguity, or errors. Preserve both until catalogs or original records resolve them.
- A dated multi-facility footprint differs from a single-facility area. Full-time workforce differs from peak or seasonal workforce.
- A founder quotation differs from an assistant forecast. A service anecdote differs from a policy. Brand positioning differs from a measured result.

Do not repair by majority vote, compromise year, repeated assistant assertion, or silent name normalization. A future verified result needs its own evidence and versioned record.

For explicit issue questions, use the separate [reputation workflow](reputation-resilience-forge.md). Keep the issue, affected entity/product, event and last-check dates, evidence type, precise procedural status, separately sourced response/remediation, uncertainty, and update trigger distinct. A forum report is not a recall or adjudication. Keep full KNOW17 details restricted until relevant review.
