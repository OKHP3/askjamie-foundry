This addendum preserves the value assessment after full rendered text became available. It does not replace the initial capped-reader provenance. Seventeen KNOW captures contain 2,955,244 rendered characters, with five explicit user and five assistant display boundaries per thread. The complete observed rendered text and link lists are privately preserved and hashed. Hidden branches, original-file identity, research sidecars, and complete semantic review remain unknown or unfinished.

The review was bounded structural and selective semantic triage: compare topic scope with the existing corpus, inspect question patterns and source leads, identify useful operating needs, and examine closing claims and speculative material. It was not a line-by-line review of all research prose. A file being captured does not make its assertions canonical.

## Claims that failed promotion

Several expansions claim to be verified and up to date under the label CanonSeal. Those are assistant assertions, not independent evidence. Scenario products, possible future programs, unexplained demographic estimates, and performance or loyalty claims require source review. KNOW07's speculative future-product wording is an explicit reason to retain a proposal/fact boundary. Question phrasing that presupposes a product, medical benefit, program, or superiority claim does not establish it.

## Effort and stopping decision

The useful first packet now includes portable baseline topics, concrete operating drafts, a question bank, topic-specific review needs, primary-source leads, and complete observed rendered captures privately. Deeper research review remains recoverable from those files. It requires a separate bounded pass by topic and decision impact, with facts verified before current use.

No total-value denominator or greater-than-60-percent/greater-than-80-percent semantic retention estimate is established. This is a partial harvest with explicit remaining value. Sources are not exhausted merely because their text was recovered.
