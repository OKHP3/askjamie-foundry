# Career evidence workflow

These portable companions work with one person's authorized career evidence.
Each has a separate trigger and produces an artifact the owner can inspect.
They can be used in sequence or on their own with suitable inputs.

| Candidate | Repeatable decision and output | Refinement and disposition |
|---|---|---|
| [Career source intake](../skills/okhp3-career-source-intake/SKILL.md) | Decide what may be inspected, where it belongs, and how to preserve its origin. Produce a private inventory, filing proposal, and exclusions. | Added purpose and use fields, partial-access reporting, archive boundaries, exact-duplicate rules, and path verification. Release as a companion. |
| [Career evidence canon](../skills/okhp3-career-evidence-canon/SKILL.md) | Decide which atomic facts can be reused as of a date. Produce a ledger with provenance, lifecycle, and unresolved conflicts. | Added source lineage, effective versus capture dates, explicit owner corrections, and separate factual and disclosure states. Release as a companion. The [résumé discrepancy audit](https://github.com/OKHP3/askjamie-foundry/issues/151) has a separate read-only scope. |
| Role-specific résumé packet | Match exact opening requirements to supported career claims. Produce traceable draft wording and identify gaps. | Keep the evidence-to-draft contract with the [targeted résumé skill](../skills/okhp3-targeted-resume/SKILL.md). The [referral handoff work](https://github.com/OKHP3/askjamie-foundry/issues/162) has its own scope. |
| [Career portfolio curation](../skills/okhp3-career-portfolio-curation/SKILL.md) | Explain an individual's contribution with defensible evidence. Produce a private case study and, on request, a separate disclosure candidate. | Added prototype and test status, transitive privacy checks, and exact-revision, audience, and destination binding. Release as a companion for an individual's portfolio. [Professional Portfolio](../../professional-portfolio/skills/professional-portfolio/SKILL.md) covers business presentation. |

A setup wizard and downloadable archive are delivery surfaces for the method.
Social copy can start from a cleared artifact and belongs in Skillz's

## Handoff boundaries

1. **Intake to canon.** Pass an approved inventory and selected source
   regions. An inventory entry can be pending, inaccessible, or excluded.
2. **Canon to role drafting.** Pass the usable claim statements and IDs,
   support status, dates, approved source IDs and exact private locators,
   permitted uses, and relevant gaps. Keep the posting in the requirements
   lane and source locators in the private mapping. A resulting résumé is
   derived content. The [candidate dossier protocol](candidate-canon-regeneration-protocol.md)
   also consumes a claim ledger and produces a larger retrieval document.
3. **Canon to case study.** Pass approved project claims and project artifacts.
   A private draft can retain a private ledger. A disclosure candidate is
   constructed separately from cleared material.

Public source consists of newly authored methods and fictional development
fixtures. Keep real records, private paths, account data, and user-generated
outputs in the owner's private workspace.
