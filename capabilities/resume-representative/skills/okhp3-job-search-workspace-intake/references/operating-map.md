# Operating map

Use one section per record type in the private handoff. Omit data the recipient lacks authority to receive.

| Section | Required fields |
|---|---|
| Review scope | As-of time and zone, account scope, host, intended audience, permitted sources, excluded or unavailable surfaces |
| System and owner map | Surface, purpose, responsible owner or gap, verified locator or safe source label, date and coverage, claim types supported |
| Workspace proposal | Existing primary folder, proposed primary folder, secondary references, device-specific checks, affected settings and evidence links |
| Opportunity and dependency queue | Existing record or provisional key, employer, title, requisition or source ID if known, status and evidence, next action, actor, timing type and basis |
| Discrepancy register | Competing statements with locators and dates, what each source establishes, unresolved question, consequence, resolving owner or smallest evidence check |
| Identity handoff | Fact, criterion, or master pointer, approving owner, version or date, scope, restrictions and conflicts, return route for corrections |

Use the owner map to distinguish source roles. A candidate portal or receipt can establish a particular submission event. Recruiter correspondence can establish what the recruiter said or offered. A calendar invitation can establish a scheduled event and its response status. A tracker establishes its own recorded stage. Approved career facts and criteria come from their designated authority. Track observed and effective dates separately when they differ.

Use statuses for coverage consistently. **Verified** describes a claim directly supported within its stated scope. **Reported** attributes a claim to an owner or historical source. **Blocked** identifies an attempted check and its access failure. **Unknown** identifies unresolved or unchecked evidence. **Proposed** identifies a future route or change.

Count each type of record independently. For partial coverage, label totals as observed and identify exclusions. Link distinct sources to an existing work item only when verified identifiers support the relationship. Flag ambiguous matches and leave the underlying records intact. Preserve original locators when a new device path is unverified.

For file evidence, capture purpose, readable locator, approval/version if applicable, and linkage to an attempt if established. Require a confirmed final submission and evidence tying the retained version to that event for the same attempt. Use a final manifest or a portal copy identified as the submitted attachment. A saved upload may have been replaced or removed before submission. Record exact submitted version as unknown when the final attachment linkage is missing. Give the archive, draft, master and receipt separate labels.

Separate the proposed queue into research or drafting and actions requiring authority. For an external or record change, identify the exact target, actor, evidence and approval still needed. A report delivered to a broader audience should use safe source labels and dates in place of private locators.
