# Architecture and evidence boundary

Outcome: help a candidate identify role-success conditions and ask proportionate, stage-appropriate resource questions before acceptance.

Inputs: role and stage, known outcomes and environment, candidate constraints, and requested scope. Output: prioritized questions, known/unknown ledger, non-negotiable issues and decision follow-up. Do not invent facts or ask every question when a concise set fits.

Knowledge advantage: distinguishes delivery authority from management title; connects AI tool entitlement, agent execution, spending, approvals, hardware and governance to outcomes; separates standard access, exceptions, funding and personal-use authorization; reserves important confirmations for the offer stage.

Boundary: preparation only. Does not make commitments, submit, send, or disclose career records. Current policy or legal claims require suitable current sources. Interview intelligence, cue cards and application status have distinct owners.

Client contract: plain portable text. No vendor, connector, file access or network is mandatory; mark unavailable information unknown. Validate synthetic development cases; independent review and live outcome measurement are separate.
