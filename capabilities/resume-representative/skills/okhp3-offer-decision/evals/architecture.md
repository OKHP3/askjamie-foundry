# Architecture and evidence boundary

Outcome: help a candidate compare actual offer terms and select a small set of negotiation priorities using documented assumptions and personal tradeoffs.

Inputs: known offer terms, status, preferences and non-negotiables, tax assumptions or jurisdiction, incremental expenses, reimbursements, and user-selected weights when available. Output: side-by-side evidence table, transparent arithmetic, gated ranking or explicitly incomplete assessment, and negotiation choices.

Knowledge advantage: distinguishes work costs paid after tax from gross pay; amortizes hardware and same-period one-time reimbursement; treats unknowns and non-negotiable prerequisites independently of weighted scores; distinguishes entitlement from prohibited personal tools and one-time bonuses from recurring expenses.

Boundary: decision support, not legal, tax, benefits, or investment advice; no sending, accepting, or tracker mutation. Do not fabricate offers, taxes, policy, employment classification, or data access.

Client contract: plain portable text and arithmetic; network is optional unless current legal or market claims are requested. Preserve missing values and identify qualified advice as appropriate. Synthetic development cases are evaluation design, not live benchmarks.
