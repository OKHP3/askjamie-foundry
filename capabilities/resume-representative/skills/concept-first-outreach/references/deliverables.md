# Deliverable set

| Artifact | Touch | Purpose | Format guidance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concept brief | First | Starts the conversation | Document, plus a version that previews without download (commonly PDF) |
| Deck | Second, on request | Supports a live or async discussion | 12 to 16 slides, speaker notes carry sources |
| Role-specific résumé | Second, on request | Answers the fit and title-gap question | Leads with the vision for the role and states the gap plainly |

## Résumé rules for a title gap

- State the gap once, early, without apology, then show comparable leadership:
  teams led, scope integrated, programs co-led, decisions owned.
- Map experience to the role's stated responsibilities using supplied evidence
  only.
- Include proof of work the reader can check, such as public projects.
- Keep out anything the candidate has chosen to keep private, even when it
  would strengthen the résumé. Ask rather than assume.

## Consolidation

When drafts of the same artifacts exist in several places or tools:

1. Inventory every candidate file with its location, date, and author tool.
2. Choose the strongest content for each canonical artifact on merit, not
   recency.
3. Move everything else into a dated archive folder. Do not delete.
4. After any upload or sync, list the destination and confirm exactly the
   canonical files are present. Sync clients can resurrect archived files or
   create duplicate copies; archive those again and tell the candidate.
5. Verify names, dates, employers, and titles in the final files against the
   candidate's evidence. List anything still unconfirmed.
