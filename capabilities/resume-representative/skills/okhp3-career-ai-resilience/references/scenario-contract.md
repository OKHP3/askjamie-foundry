# Career AI Resilience scenario contract

## Claim tiers

- `verified`: inspected primary record directly supports the narrow claim
- `self-reported`: owner account, not independently corroborated
- `inferred`: reasoned interpretation requiring visible premises
- `hypothesis`: scenario driver or testable future proposition
- `unknown`: not established

## Example (entirely fictional)

Subject: an experienced distribution-systems architect seeking accountable automation roles. A decade of delivery is self-reported, not verified. No compensation baseline or live market data supplied.

5-year favorable: increased demand for cross-system integration; requires proof of agent governance outcomes. 5-year adverse: automation compresses general architecture consulting; the candidate needs differentiated domain outcomes. 25-year: only broad conditional scenarios. Do not assign percentiles or salary milestones.

Experiment: document one demonstrable synthetic process redesign with quantified test results from a real or controlled benchmark, then request an external reviewer to inspect the evidence. Outcome: a verifiable portfolio artifact, not guaranteed employment.

## Forecast safeguards

A salary indexed to 100 is not automatically $100,000. Explicitly label all indexes as normalized. Keep 'real' purchasing-power and 'nominal' wages separate. A cohort earning profile is not an individual person's earnings path. Past earnings trajectories do not establish future AI outcomes. Unverified market numbers must remain unverified even if cited by an older generated summary.

For each scenario: assumption, support tier, horizon, possible counterevidence, response trigger and bounded recommendation. Never infer individual cognitive decline from age.
