## Earning capacity levers

The plausible levers to investigate are ownership of consequential outcomes, relevant domain knowledge, verified delivery, effective stakeholder coordination and access to employers that reward them. These are hypotheses to test through R5 and R6, not confirmed routes to higher earnings.

An eventual economic comparison should keep six questions separate:

1. **Income level:** what dated compensation is actually available for comparable work?
2. **Nominal wage growth:** how might cash pay change over a stated period?
3. **Real purchasing power:** what inflation assumption is applied, and why?
4. **Transition cost:** what learning time, fees or lost earnings would a move require?
5. **Income volatility:** how do contract duration, variable pay, benefits and working hours differ?
6. **Employment access and continuity:** what evidence supports Morgan's ability to obtain and sustain the work?

No salary projection or normalized earnings index is calculated here. C3, M2 and M3 are missing. Agent count, tool familiarity and a job title do not establish business impact or earnings. A higher advertised range would not establish that Morgan qualifies, receives that offer or improves total realized income.
