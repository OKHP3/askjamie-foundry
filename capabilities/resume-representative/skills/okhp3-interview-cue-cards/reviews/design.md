# Design and evaluation contract

Outcome: convert a question, an actual candidate answer, and authorized career evidence into concise, source-grounded cues and actionable practice feedback.

Inputs: candidate answer or evidence, target question or role, desired mode and disclosure restrictions. Output: a compact cue card with evidence anchors, boundaries and a next practice prompt. Missing evidence stays missing.

Scope: private preparation and review. Excludes taking an assessment on the candidate's behalf, live interview impersonation, fabricated production incidents, application submission and outreach. Candidate controls practice pace, tone and disclosure.

Knowledge advantage: separate assessment prompt parts; map exact evidence to claims; distinguish actual enterprise proofs of concept, production incident and proposed architecture; preserve deployment scope and practitioner authorship; break a lengthy answer into priorities, failure conditions and evidence before release; treat organizational adoption funding and ownership as evidence only when supplied.

Portable core takes supplied inputs. Search, saving and runtime integrations are optional and require separately scoped permissions. Predeclared acceptance: all four development cases satisfy every expectation, safety failures block release; independent release review and unseen holdout remain external. No task-quality or uplift claim without matched live runs.
