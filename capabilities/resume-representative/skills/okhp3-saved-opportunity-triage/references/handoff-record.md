# Saved opportunity handoff record

## Review summary

Record tracker or export, observed time, pages or cursor coverage, and any access gap. Count distinct listing keys. Use source plus posting ID or a verified stable URL. Show unkeyed observations separately. Separately report the count of verified listed employer groups and, when checked, verified active listings and their employers. If a row has an unresolved employer, show it separately and exclude it from the verified employer count. A saved record can refer to a posting that has closed.

## Opening table

Use one row per distinct listing. Link listings to the same work item only if their exact employer and requisition match.

## Employer brief

- Employer, verified aliases, staffing firm or client relationship where applicable, and existing owner or proposed thread label.
- Exact roles, stable IDs and links, source observation dates, priority rationale, and unresolved criteria. Keep one work item per confirmed employer and requisition even when several ads point to it.
- Existing source packets, approved career facts to consult, and verified role folder. Mark suggested locations as proposed. Reuse a folder only after matching its exact identity.
- Next authorized action per role, with owner. Distinguish a prepared handoff from a created thread, drafted document, or submitted application.

## Dated handoff refresh

Keep the historical opening table as observed. Record its source, as-of time,
coverage, listing keys, employer counts, rank and state. Give the refresh its
own as-of time and source coverage. Preserve the historical rows even if the
role later closed, moved, or was submitted. Add newly discovered roles only
to a separately dated cohort. Use supplied evidence offline. If a needed source
or companion workflow is unavailable, name the missing check and keep that
field unresolved.

| Verified employer and requisition, or provisional posting key | Source-qualified IDs or stable URLs | Claim, candidate account, route and attempt where applicable, source, event and observation dates | Legacy locator, verified current locator and identity basis, or unresolved | Existing owner and evidence link, or owner gap | Exact role material and its state, action owner and date basis |

- Consume Application Status Reconciliation results through a verified opening
  crosswalk. Assign route- or attempt-specific claims only to unique matches.
  Keep availability, application attempts and dispositions, and owner pursuit
  decisions independent. Preserve conflicts and record missing dates or sources.
  A later tracker label alone cannot establish an employer outcome.
- Use Career Artifact Audit for exact legacy-to-current links and Career File
  Routing to verify the role's current destination. Record the folder identity
  basis and whether it was verified on the local or provider surface. Keep
  ambiguous, stale, or inaccessible locators unresolved. A renamed path or
  same-name replacement by itself cannot establish continuity. Use Workspace
  Intake or owner-controlled evidence to locate the existing coordinator and
  employer owner. Keep proposed owners and paths explicitly provisional.
- Prepare one kickoff brief per requested employer. Include the shared employer
  context once and give each requisition its own posting, verified destination,
  role requirements, approved career sources, packet state, and next action.
  Repeated ads can share a requisition work item when the crosswalk is verified.
  Route material preparation through the role-tailoring or application-preparation
  workflow after verifying the current requirements and approved sources. Keep
  exact attempt-linked submitted materials as historical evidence. A receipt
  without a version link leaves the submitted material unknown. Route ambiguous
  or closed work to verification or closeout as supported by the evidence.
  Scope submission and disposition to the exact attempt. Give any distinct
  viable route or draft its own status and next action.
- Distinguish a prepared brief, an authorized and completed draft, and a proposed
  packet. Keep private evidence and locators within their authorized audience.
  Prepare the kickoff for the established owner. Thread creation, tracker edits,
  moving folders, uploads, outreach, and submission each require their own
  authorization and workflow.

## Tracker update receipt

For each authorized edit record the listing key, field, before and intended values or safe summaries, assessment key, readback result, and observation time. Keep private note text and personal records in the approved workspace. Use synthetic data for shared examples.
