# Architecture and evaluation intent

- **Outcome:** Map role requirements to usable learning and independently verifiable credential opportunities, then track only meaningful changes when authorized.
- **Input:** Supplied or authorized role criteria and candidate evidence, learner constraints, relevant current primary sources, and optional collection or scheduling capabilities.
- **Output:** Requirement-to-learning mapping, source-dated offer records, explicit unknowns and access status, plus separately verified authorized saves or monitor state.
- **Boundary:** Do not infer eligibility, competency, completion, a credential, payment authorization, consent, or background from course enrollment. Never take an exam or assessment for someone. Sending messages, certification registration, payment, tests, and agreement acceptance are separate actions.
- **Knowledge advantage:** Typed distinctions between required/preferred/equivalent and claimed/verified; training, badge lab, practice, and proctored exam; event, request, redemption, and exam dates; exact offer identity, eligibility, location, true total cost, and at-home delivery; no unsupported monitoring or completion claims.
- **Client contract:** The core accepts supplied snapshots without connectors. Fresh sources, collection mutations, and recurring monitoring are optional host capabilities with explicit blocked outcomes.
- **Evidence plan:** Synthetic development cases specify the workflow and safety boundaries. Versions 0.1.0 and 0.3.0 had small paired text-only pilots, now historical. Version 0.2.0 was an analytical intermediate with no live trial. Current-release results are recorded separately when available; no live host evaluation or protected unseen holdout is established. No uplift or production-readiness claim is justified.
- **Relationship:** Saved-opportunity triage owns job selection. Role-to-practice overlaps in mapping credential gaps and selecting learning, and owns hands-on practice planning. Application reconciliation owns application status. This package adds detailed offer qualification, authorized collection saves, and change-watch safeguards; use the skills together rather than repeating work.
