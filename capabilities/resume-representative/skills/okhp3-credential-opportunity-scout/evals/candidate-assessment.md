# Candidate assessment

This is a public-safe, point-in-time analytical assessment of recurring career-workflow patterns. It is not a claim of measured skill uplift or an inventory of a person's private applications. Repository and issue states were inspected on 2026-11-09 and can change.

| Candidate | Repeatable value | Existing coverage or decision | Gap and action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saved-opening triage | Compare exact listings with stated fit, location, pay and unknowns; maintain a scoped priority. | [Saved opportunity triage](https://github.com/OKHP3/askjamie-foundry/tree/main/capabilities/resume-representative/skills/okhp3-saved-opportunity-triage) is already on the default branch. | Reuse it; do not create another ranking skill. |
| Application-state reconciliation | Match exact application routes to receipts, status events, recruiter milestones and trackers. | [Application status reconciliation](https://github.com/OKHP3/askjamie-foundry/tree/main/capabilities/resume-representative/skills/okhp3-application-status-reconciliation) is already on the default branch; related enhancements are underway. | Reuse it for application status. A saved course or job is not a submission receipt. |
| Role-specific career representation | Map a person's supported experience to a role without inventing credentials, duties or results. | [Targeted resume](https://github.com/OKHP3/askjamie-foundry/tree/main/capabilities/resume-representative/skills/okhp3-targeted-resume) is on the default branch; [Resume Representative](https://github.com/OKHP3/askjamie-foundry/tree/main/capabilities/resume-representative/skills/resume-representative) remains an integration candidate. | Reuse the targeted skill and preserve the integration candidate's status; do not call host activation or behavioral performance verified. |
| Interview intelligence and answer practice | Translate recruiter guidance into likely topics, then build concise, attributed answers while distinguishing experience from hypotheticals. | [Interview evidence map](https://github.com/OKHP3/askjamie-foundry/tree/main/capabilities/resume-representative/skills/okhp3-interview-evidence-map) and [interview questions](https://github.com/OKHP3/askjamie-foundry/tree/main/capabilities/resume-representative/skills/okhp3-interview-questions) are on the default branch; separate [intelligence](https://github.com/OKHP3/askjamie-foundry/issues/161) and [cue-card](https://github.com/OKHP3/askjamie-foundry/issues/152) proposals were open when inspected. | Reuse merged coverage and leave the separately tracked proposals to their maintainers. |
| Role-to-practice planning | Turn an evidence-backed fit gap into a focused learning or practice plan. | [Role-to-practice](https://github.com/OKHP3/askjamie-foundry/tree/main/capabilities/resume-representative/skills/okhp3-role-to-practice) is on the default branch and also covers selecting learning options. | Compose with it, rather than duplicating its exercise workflow. | Submission and authorization gates | Distinguish preparation, approval, transmission and an employer receipt. | [Application authorization gates](https://github.com/OKHP3/askjamie-foundry/issues/155) were tracked when inspected. | Leave form completion to the separately authorized workflow. |
| Credential and learning opportunity scout | Distinguish role requirements, free instruction, practice assessments, skill badges and actual certification exams; verify costs, terms and ongoing availability. | No matching package was found in the inspected default-branch Resume Representative skills on 2026-11-09. | Create this narrow companion package. Its differentiator is exact-offer and eligibility evidence plus safe save/watch behavior, not generic course advice. |

Each row describes a repeatable decision with inputs, failure modes, and a verifiable output. Publication of this one package does not complete the other candidates. All proposed uses must preserve the host's current permissions and privacy boundaries.
