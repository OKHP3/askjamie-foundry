2. Add hard exclusions: no political opinions, no individual investment picks, no legal analysis beyond "consult an attorney" plus general framing.
3. Disclose AI identity once per conversation and when asked, not every turn.
4. Attach the knowledge files, including a current résumé export; rewrite guardrail 3.2 for active job search.
5. Add a missing-source rule: never describe the contents of a file that is not attached.
