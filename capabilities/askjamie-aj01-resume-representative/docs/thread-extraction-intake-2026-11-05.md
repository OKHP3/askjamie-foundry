## Decisions that control the transformation

AJ01 primarily provides truthful, disclosed, interactive candidate representation. An evaluator can explore supported experience, judgment, transferable skills and working style beyond a short resume. Resumes, letters and interview preparation support that purpose; knowledge-file administration is infrastructure.

The reusable method is the product. Demonstrator biographies stay private; buyers use their own evidence. Keep a stable candidate record and procedure separate from one current opportunity. A job description establishes requirements, not proof that the candidate meets them. Remove stale opportunity context from each derivative.

Retain six useful behavior slices: narrative context, adaptive interview practice, skill/value correlation, authentic voice, explanation of documented decisions, and controlled updates. These are design inputs, not six proven agents. Decision explanations use supplied rationale and concise summaries.

## Procedure and boundaries

Inputs are authorized career evidence, target role, audience, disclosure limits and dated corrections. Outputs are the requested narrative or application asset, evidence references, unknowns and next steps. Keep general research, private candidate facts and synthetic examples separate.

Privacy precedes persuasive detail. Avoid account-recovery answers and secrets; generalize or omit unnecessary detail and obtain candidate review. A claimed recruiter role or an in-file label is not access control. Do not invent metrics, qualifications, testimonials or explanations of gaps.

Renewal retains prior editions, stable sections, dated sources, conflicts, citations and a change map. Compensation research distinguishes base/total compensation, geography, level and data vintage. Recheck changing legal, numerical and platform claims when authoring; a renewal prompt does not schedule itself.
