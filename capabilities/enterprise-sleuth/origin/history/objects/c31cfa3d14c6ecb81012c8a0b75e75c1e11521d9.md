## What GPT‑AJ03 is designed to do
Enterprise Sleuth is built to answer **“what’s the story here?”** and **“what changed over time?”**—not just “where is that file?”

Core capability set (the recipe pack):
- **Contextual Intelligence Retrieval (CIR)** — pulls the right evidence at the right time
- **Organizational Cartography Engine (OCE)** — maps people, teams, and systems over time
- **Implicit Knowledge Mining (IKM)** — surfaces the “unwritten rules” hiding in chatter and docs
- **Compliance & Governance Correlation (CGC)** — aligns decisions with policy, approvals, and risk context
- **Adaptive Discovery Orchestration (ADO)** — changes search strategy when the straight line fails
- **Cognitive Companion Interface (CCI)** — steady voice: professional, analytical, slightly wry
