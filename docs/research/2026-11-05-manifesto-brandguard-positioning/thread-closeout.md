## Lexical and brand decision record

| Term | Appropriate purpose | Risk or limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mission statement / vision | Aspirational identity and future direction | More familiar, less distinctive |
| Manifesto | Outward-facing declaration of convictions and operating principles | Some audiences associate it with radical or confrontational rhetoric; actual copy and surrounding identity matter |
| Credo / creed | Core beliefs and commitments | Does not automatically define delivery or controls |
| Charter | Authority, scope, accountability and commitments | Sounds more formal, may imply governance approval |
| Protocol | Defined repeatable behavior, rules, validation and exceptions | Operational mechanism rather than replacement for a point-of-view declaration |
| Playbook / standard | Actionable execution guidance | Useful for buyers, but not a substitute for a founding thesis |

## Brand and enterprise-trust architecture

**Owner-stated direction in thread:** a deliberately bold OverKill Hill parent identity with a more welcoming AskJamie commercial face and a separate BrandGuard capability. Cross-links are acceptable, but BrandGuard's enterprise sales path should not make the manifesto its immediate or mandatory context.

**Repository evidence:** [AskJamie registry](https://github.com/OKHP3/askjamie-foundry/blob/e1074940a47bb3b5a306248ba501e5e9637f0edd/registry/index.yaml) identifies `aj04` BrandGuard as `draft`, `private`, `planned` and `private-external`. The public [FoundRy governance](https://github.com/OKHP3/askjamie-foundry/blob/e1074940a47bb3b5a306248ba501e5e9637f0edd/docs/governance.md) excludes client overlays and protected client source from publication. Brand separation is therefore consistent with the recorded architecture. It does **not** prove BrandGuard is already hosted beneath an AskJamie web route, nor does it establish existing customer approval.

**Actionable buyer-facing recommendation:** BrandGuard must earn trust on its own merits: clearly define what IP it reads, how brand assets are accessed, retained and segregated, how permissions and approvals work, how audit trails and review/rollback are handled, and what an engagement actually delivers. Support claims with verified demos, controlled examples and policies, not with rhetorical distance from the parent brand alone. Design the public buying journey around safeguards and outcomes; keep creative provenance accessible but optional. A buyer can still follow cross-links and perform due diligence.
