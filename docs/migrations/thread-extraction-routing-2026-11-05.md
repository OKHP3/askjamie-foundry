## Shared inputs to reuse

The reusable method is the product. Legacy instructions and examples become skill procedures, references and output contracts. Host-specific behavior belongs in adapters with explicit dependencies and losses.

Separate stable evidence/procedures from the current engagement. Candidate/business records establish facts; job descriptions/RFPs establish needs; editorial notes establish emphasis.

Reuse the shared flywheel: specification, versioned source, curated packs, experimentation, demonstrations and feedback. Maintain one authoritative identity/version/link record. A pipeline design does not prove synchronization or publication.

Renewal preserves prior editions, stable retrieval sections, dated evidence, conflicts, scenarios and change maps. Keep research, personal evidence and voice inference distinct. Existing commercial models separate discovery, checkout, fulfillment, seller proceeds and buyer totals; historical prices or platform claims are not accepted product facts.

AskJamie is an ecosystem under OverKill Hill, with Glee-fully as a peer; Foundry is the tool-making system. Enterprise examples and research studies do not expand an AJ01/AJ02 kit's included scope. Use accepted brand assets and retain unresolved image/typography decisions.

## Integration and de-duplication rules

1. Keep issue 58 as the AJ01/AJ02 umbrella and issue 39 as the broader gated
   backlog. Add a bounded update to the existing record instead of another
   master tracker.
2. Integrate a missing behavior or document delta through its existing owner.
   A matching topic, absent assignee or old timestamp does not transfer custody.
3. Keep one migration obligation ledger. This index and capability intakes link
   to it; they do not maintain competing completion counts.
4. Preserve dated records and pinned origin snapshots. Add a current status
   overlay to the maintained record rather than rewrite historical evidence.
5. Compare source scope, actual patches and provenance before consolidating
   similar work. Repeated headings or matching hashes alone do not authorize
   deletion or establish that unique requirements are covered.
6. Treat branch or issue retirement as a proposal until a current authorization
   names the exact targets. Apply the branch-lifecycle gates: preserve unique
   work; retain protected/deployment branches and active-owner/worktree
   dependencies. Remote branch deletion requires an expected-tip conditional
   operation; a fresh reread alone is insufficient. File moves, deletion and
   history rewriting also require their exact approved target set.

## Source closeout and next handoff

For this rapid-extraction pass, assess source closure after distinctive accessible content and material assets are retained, capture limits are honest, receiving custody is assigned and no active worker depends on it. Unknown alternate branches alone are not a material gap. Missing unique text/assets require recovery or explicit custodian disposition.

Transform preserved material without recreating completed packages, and hand any remaining specific dependency to its existing owner.

Skill acceptance, research currency, rights, host behavior, publication and retirement remain separately evidenced stages. Conversation archival does not retire other source surfaces.
