# AJ01/AJ02 migration disposition addendum — 2026-10-30

## Purpose and record boundary

This standalone addendum records the owner-reported disposition of the AJ01
and AJ02 child Replit projects and the limits of their recovered Git history.
It supplements, and does not replace or amend, the dated migration, comparison,
closeout, ledger, or evidence records linked below. The preserved source
snapshots remain under their FoundRy capability paths. FoundRy is the intended
home for both capabilities.

## Owner decision

Jamie confirms that both child Replit projects have been deleted and accepts
their unrecovered local and platform-managed state as unrecovered. This is an
accepted-loss disposition, not evidence that the state was backed up or
recoverable. It applies only to those child projects and their unrecovered
state; it does not establish deletion or disposition of either GitHub source
repository, either GPT, website paths, or Notion anchors.
