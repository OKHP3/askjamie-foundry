# Interview preparation: skill extraction and review

Date: 2026-11-09
Decision scope: public distribution of draftable source with analytical review.

## What became reusable

| Candidate | Why it qualifies as a skill | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Interview evidence map | Repeatedly converts a role and career sources into defensible personal claims, story choices, transferable-platform explanations and explicit qualification gaps. Its contribution taxonomy prevents several plausible misrepresentations. | Package as `okhp3-interview-evidence-map`. |
| Interview questions | Repeatedly preserves a candid concern while separating observation, personal experience and unsupported premise, then asks for a concrete example. It produces a spoken question, follow-up and coaching note. | Package as `okhp3-interview-questions`. |
| Public work as career evidence | Distinguishes delivery proof, method, experiment and visibility; connects an artifact to a role without implying deployment, endorsement or hiring impact. It shares the first package's evidence model. | Include a focused public-work reference in the evidence-map package. |
| Platform experience translation | Connects architecture decisions to an unfamiliar platform without inventing implementation experience or turning company publicity into job prerequisites. | Include in the evidence-map package. |
| Thread transfer and process modeling | Valuable workflows already have dedicated extraction, handoff, process-capture and Mermaid contracts. A host lookup failure supplies no new permission or transfer mechanism. | Reuse existing packages; no duplicate skill. |

These are skills because they have recurring inputs, domain-specific decisions,
observable outputs and meaningful failure boundaries. The source conversation
motivated the method; it did not prove the finished packages improve outcomes.

## What the original workflow lacked

The conversation had useful judgment but depended on shared context. Packaging
adds explicit subject and opportunity identity, a source ledger, evidence types,
an attribution boundary, qualification checks, missing-input behavior, a public
drafting handoff and versioned evaluation cases. It also keeps the honest
investment question intact while removing unsupported criticism and praise.

Equilibrium Review used three independent static contexts: evidence, outcome,
and safety/portability. All recommended `approve-with-limits` for draftable
source. Their common limits were absent behavioral execution, trigger evidence,
unseen holdout and pending version-specific review records. The conditional
disruptor and adjudication are recorded in each package's review JSON.

The disruptor identified contradictory career sources as an untested edge.
The final evidence-map contract now preserves both records, applies explicit
owner corrections as supplied testimony, and holds unresolved fields unknown.
An eighth development case covers conflicting duration and ownership claims.

The release records close the documentation gap. They do not close the live
evidence gap. All public cases are author-visible development cases. No measured
uplift, interviewer reaction, causal hiring effect or universal host acceptance
is claimed.

## Canonical source and composition

Canonical packages live under `capabilities/resume-representative/skills/` in
AskJamie Foundry. Exact core mirrors live in the Skillz `askjamie/` family.
They are interview-preparation contracts, so their home is the career capability
rather than a platform-specific social-posting family. Existing LinkedIn or
other social skills can draft an explicitly requested post from the public-safe
handoff. Neither interview skill grants publishing or outreach authority.

The existing Resume Representative core and immutable imported source remain
unchanged. Private interview history, employer details, calendar and account
locators are omitted; fixtures are synthetic.
